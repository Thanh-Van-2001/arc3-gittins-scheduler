--- Sergei Makarov (votes {'canUpvote': True}): I think this is a textbook game-theory setup, and the hosts designed it this way on purpose.

The top places are very close. Whoever open-sources gets the milestone money, and gets competition along with it: everyone else starts building on their work. Whoever keeps it closed gets no milestone money, and still faces the same competition the moment someone else publishes. Staying closed doesn't protect you if another top team can open up anyway.

For everyone below the prize places it makes no difference at all: as soon as an open solution appears, the whole leaderboard moves up at once, for everybody equally.

So as I see it, this is exactly what the milestone is for: the spirit of the competition and shared progress. The milestone prize is the price the hosts pay to make the best ideas public while the competition is still running.
