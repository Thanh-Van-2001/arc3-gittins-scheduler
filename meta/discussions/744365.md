# Hill-climbing on the LB happens in groups?  (id 744365, votes None, by Nick Pellegrin)

I've noticed this phenomenon a few times in several kaggle competitions before, where the Leaderboard stagnates for a while until one team suddenly finds an unlock and jumps up in score, which is then closely followed by a handlful of other teams making huge gains as well. It makes sense that over the course of a competition, the top scores act like a stepwise function for every new unlock, but it's always stood out to me how the jumps seem to happen for a group of teams all at once.🤣

I'm not suggesting there is any secretive sharing of solutions behind the scenes, my guess is that seeing a top team jump up proves that further improvements are possible and motivates other teams to crack down and surpass. Watching the race up to Milestone #2 has been wild, it seems like every 5 hours there's another huge jump. I remember being excited to finally start hitting above 4%... 

What do you guys think? Is there some game-theory explanation that a handful of teams seem to discover a big improvement around the same time, or just lucky coincidence? I just find it really fascinating to watch.
